Synchronization in Selenium WebDriver

90% of flakiness in automation comes from poor synchronization. Scripts run faster than the browser can render. Waits bridge this gap.

Why Synchronization is needed?

When you click a button, the page might take 2 seconds to load data via AJAX. If your script tries to verify the data immediately, it fails with `NoSuchElementException`.

Implicit Wait

A global setting. It tells WebDriver to wait for a certain amount of time before throwing a `NoSuchElementException`. 

driver.manage().timeouts().implicitlyWait(Duration.ofSeconds(10));

Downside: It waits for the element to exist in DOM, not necessarily be visible/interactive.

Explicit Wait

Wait for a specific condition (visible, clickable) on a specific element. This is the recommended approach. 

WebDriverWait wait = new WebDriverWait(driver, Duration.ofSeconds(10));
wait.until(ExpectedConditions.visibilityOfElementLocated(By.id("username")));

Fluent Wait

More advanced. You can define polling interval and exceptions to ignore.
